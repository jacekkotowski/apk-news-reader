## Stash Reader 0.9: share to Stash, and recall

**New**
- **Share to Stash.** In YouTube, Chrome, a newsletter app or anywhere with a Share button, choose **Stash Reader**. The link lands in your Stash straight away (title from the share, or from the page), with a small "Saved to Stash" message. A shared YouTube link gets the Watch button like any video. Items saved this way come from a source called "Shared with Stash Reader".
- **Recall.** Your highlights come back as short questions, so what you read sticks. At the top of the Stash a button says "Recall: N cards for today". Each card asks "What do you remember about this?" with the title of the article, you think, press **Show answer**, then **Got it** or **Again tomorrow**. Cards come back after 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days, and **Again** starts that item over. At most 5 cards a day, so the session ends: "Done for today". Highlights you already made get a card too. Removing a highlight removes its card.

**Try on the phone**
1. In YouTube press Share on a video and pick Stash Reader. Then the same from Chrome on an article. Look in the Stash.
2. Highlight a passage in a kept article; the next day (or after the first Recall appears) do a Recall session.

**Known limits**
- Nothing here has been tested on a real phone. The emulator test sends one shared link and shows that the app takes it into the Stash; it cannot press the Share button of other apps or judge how Recall feels.
- Recall only has highlights to ask about so far. Notes you write are not turned into cards.
- The first card of a new highlight is due the next day.
- Earlier limits still apply: text selection, the share sheet out, the export folder, page watch, recommendations, podcasts and videos are untested on a phone.
