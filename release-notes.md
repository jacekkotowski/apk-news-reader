## Stash Reader 0.5.1: Keep button fix

**Fixed**
- **Keep** was drawn as a filled blue button, so it looked already pressed, and pressing it made the whole card disappear. Now Keep is a plain button like Done. After you press it the card stays open and shows **"✓ Kept in your Stash"** with an **Undo** button. The card leaves the Feed the next time the list is drawn, and it is in the Stash tab straight away.
- Highlighting a passage shows the same "Kept" state.
- In the Stash tab the button is **Remove from Stash**.

**Try on the phone:** open a Feed card, press Keep, check the green Kept line, press Undo, press Keep again, open the Stash tab.

**Known limits:** as in 0.5. Nothing has been tested on a real phone; text selection, the share sheet and the export folder are still untested.
