## Stash Reader 1.1: fewer buttons

**Changed** (a clean-up, no new features)
- **An opened card has three buttons, not up to seven:** **Keep** (it turns into "✓ Kept"; press again to undo), one main button for the content (**Read full article**, **▶ Watch** or **▶ Listen**), and **⋯**. The ⋯ row holds the rest: **Make notes** (only when you saved a Gemini key), **Open in browser**, **Hide** and **Less like this**.
- **"Done" is now called "Hide".** It only removes the card from your Feed. **Less like this** hides it too and also ranks similar items lower; Keep is the opposite and ranks similar items higher. Hide and Less like this show only for items in the Feed that you have not kept.
- **No "Read full article" button when the feed already sent the whole text** (the label above the text says so).
- **Text size moved** from every opened card to Sources ("Reading text size").
- **The Gemini block is at the bottom of Sources.**
- **In the Stash the export buttons are folded away** behind one line, "Export folder: ... Options".
- Double-tap is not used: on Android it selects a word, which highlighting needs.

**Try on the phone:** open a few cards (an article, a video, a podcast) and check the three buttons and the ⋯ row; open Sources and check the order; open the Stash and press Options.

**Known limits**
- Nothing here has been tested on a real phone. The layout was driven in a browser against a stand-in for the app, and the emulator test only shows the app starts and Keep works.
- Earlier limits still apply.
